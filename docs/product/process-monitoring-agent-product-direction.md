# Process Monitoring Agent Product Direction

## Decision

Do not position this product as generic productivity software.

Position it as:

`Personal security and device visibility dashboard`

This is the stronger direction because it fits:

1. the existing `process-monitoring-agent` codebase
2. your cybersecurity positioning
3. a clearer and more differentiated market
4. a more believable product story for SaaS and freelance use

## The 2 Options We Compared

### 1. Personal Productivity

This means:

- tracking app usage
- focus time
- distraction reports
- screen time patterns
- work session analytics

Core promise:

`Help me understand where my time goes and improve focus.`

Why we did not choose it:

- broad and crowded market
- weaker fit with your current repo
- weaker fit with your cybersecurity brand
- harder to stand out against existing tools

### 2. Personal Security / Device Visibility

This means:

- seeing what is running on your devices
- detecting unusual or suspicious processes
- monitoring device health and changes
- tracking online/offline state and process history
- keeping a private audit trail of device activity

Core promise:

`See what is happening on your devices, detect unusual activity early, and keep a private record of device health and process changes.`

Why we chose it:

- strong fit with `process-monitoring-agent`
- strong fit with your cybersecurity positioning
- sharper and more differentiated market angle
- clearer path to a small SaaS product

## Final Product Framing

Recommended positioning:

`Personal Security and Device Visibility Dashboard`

One-line product idea:

`A privacy-first dashboard that shows what your devices are doing, what changed, and what needs attention.`

## What The Product Should Be

The product should help a user answer:

1. Which devices are online right now?
2. What processes are running on them?
3. What changed today?
4. Is anything unusual or suspicious?
5. Which device needs attention?

## MVP Feature Set

The first useful version should include:

1. device list
2. device online/offline status
3. latest process snapshot per device
4. process history
5. CPU, memory, and storage summary
6. alerts for new or unusual processes
7. audit trail of important device changes
8. simple dashboard for all devices

## Audience

Best initial audience:

1. developers
2. freelancers
3. founders
4. privacy-conscious users
5. users with multiple laptops/desktops

Possible later audience:

1. small teams
2. agencies
3. MSP-style small ops teams

## What To Avoid In Version 1

Do not build this as:

1. parental surveillance software
2. employee monitoring spyware
3. full mobile deep-monitoring product
4. enterprise observability platform

## Product Principles

Version 1 should be:

1. privacy-first
2. web-first for the workspace and desktop-first for the agent footprint
3. focused on your own devices
4. lightweight to install
5. useful without enterprise complexity

## Mobile Reality

Desktop support is the best fit first:

1. Mac
2. Windows
3. Linux

Mobile support should be limited later because OS restrictions are much tighter, especially on iPhone.

## Why This Can Become A SaaS

Yes, this can become a SaaS if positioned correctly.

The right SaaS angle is not:

`full observability`

The right SaaS angle is:

`lightweight personal endpoint visibility`

or

`privacy-first device security dashboard`

## Potential Markets

The clearest market opportunities are:

1. personal device visibility for power users
2. security-aware monitoring for freelancers and founders
3. small personal device fleets
4. lightweight endpoint visibility for small teams later

## Product Naming Directions

Possible names:

1. `DeviceWatch`
2. `HostLens`
3. `ProcessGuard`
4. `NodeWatch`
5. `DevicePulse`
6. `Local Sentinel`

## Strategic Conclusion

The best direction is:

`Personal security and device visibility`

Build the product around:

1. visibility
2. change detection
3. lightweight alerts
4. privacy
5. control over your own devices

This is a better fit than generic productivity software and gives you a more credible, differentiated product story.
